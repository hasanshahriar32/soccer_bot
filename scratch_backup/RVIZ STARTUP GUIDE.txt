### Here is the 10-second fix:

  1. Look near your Windows clock (bottom right system tray). If you see the XLaunch icon, right-click it and select Exit.
  2. Open XLaunch from your Start Menu again.
  3. Click Next -> Next.
  4. ⚠️ On the "Extra settings" screen, CHECK the box: Disable access control!
  (Also check "Native opengl" if it's there).
  5. Click Next -> Finish.
  6. Windows Firewall Popup: If a Windows Firewall prompt pops up, check BOTH "Private" and "Public" networks, then click Allow
  access.
  ──────
  Now go back to your Ubuntu terminal and run:

    export DISPLAY=$(ip route show default | awk '{print $3}'):0
    export LIBGL_ALWAYS_INDIRECT=0
    source /opt/ros/jazzy/setup.bash
    rviz2

  The native rviz2 window will immediately open on your screen!
