1. ARDUINO UNO + L298N + 2 × JGA25-370 ENCODER MOTOR WIRING MATRIX
================================================================================

[A] LEFT WHEEL MOTOR (6-Pin JGA25-370 Connector):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Motor Pin 1 (M1)          --> Connect to L298N OUT1 (Left Screw Terminal)
  Motor Pin 2 (Encoder GND) --> Connect to Arduino GND Pin
  Motor Pin 3 (Encoder A/C1)--> Connect to Arduino Digital Pin D2 (Top Row)
  Motor Pin 4 (Encoder B/C2)--> Connect to Arduino Digital Pin D3 (Top Row)
  Motor Pin 5 (Encoder VCC) --> Connect to Arduino 5V Pin (5V ONLY for Encoder)
  Motor Pin 6 (M2)          --> Connect to L298N OUT2 (Left Screw Terminal)

[B] RIGHT WHEEL MOTOR (6-Pin JGA25-370 Connector):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Motor Pin 1 (M1)          --> Connect to L298N OUT3 (Right Screw Terminal)
  Motor Pin 2 (Encoder GND) --> Connect to Arduino GND Pin
  Motor Pin 3 (Encoder A/C1)--> Connect to Arduino Digital Pin D4 (Top Row)
  Motor Pin 4 (Encoder B/C2)--> Connect to Arduino Digital Pin D7 (Top Row)
  Motor Pin 5 (Encoder VCC) --> Connect to Arduino 5V Pin (Share with Left Motor 5V)
  Motor Pin 6 (M2)          --> Connect to L298N OUT4 (Right Screw Terminal)

[C] L298N MOTOR DRIVER TO ARDUINO UNO (Logic & Speed Pins):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Arduino D5  --> Connect to L298N ENA (Left Motor PWM Speed)
  Arduino D9  --> Connect to L298N IN1 (Left Motor Direction A)
  Arduino D10 --> Connect to L298N IN2 (Left Motor Direction B)

  Arduino D6  --> Connect to L298N ENB (Right Motor PWM Speed)
  Arduino D11 --> Connect to L298N IN3 (Right Motor Direction A)
  Arduino D12 --> Connect to L298N IN4 (Right Motor Direction B)

  * L298N JUMPER SETTINGS:
    - If connecting D5 & D6 to ENA & ENB: Remove the black jumper caps from ENA/ENB.
    - If NOT using D5 & D6: Keep the black jumper caps ON ENA and ENB (fixed 100% speed).

[D] POWER & COMMON GROUND CONNECTIONS (L298N 3-Pin Screw Terminal):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Battery (+) [7.4V - 12V]  --> Connect to L298N +12V / VS Screw Terminal
  Battery (-) [GND]         --> Connect to L298N GND Screw Terminal
  Arduino GND Jumper Wire   --> Connect to L298N GND Screw Terminal (COMMON GND)
  5V Regulator Jumper Cap   --> Keep ON (small black cap behind screw terminals)
  Arduino Uno Power         --> USB Cable connected to Raspberry Pi
