1. ARDUINO UNO & L298N PINOUT & WIRING MATRIX
================================================================================

[A] LEFT WHEEL MOTOR (6-Pin Connector):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Motor Pin 1 (M+)   --> Connect to L298N OUT1 (Left Screw Terminal)
  Motor Pin 2 (GND)  --> Connect to Arduino GND Pin (Bottom Row, Power Header)
  Motor Pin 3 (A)    --> Connect to Arduino Digital Pin 2 (Top Row)
  Motor Pin 4 (B)    --> Connect to Arduino Digital Pin 4 (Top Row)
  Motor Pin 5 (VCC)  --> Connect to Arduino 5V Pin (Bottom Row, Power Header)
  Motor Pin 6 (M-)   --> Connect to L298N OUT2 (Left Screw Terminal)

[B] RIGHT WHEEL MOTOR (6-Pin Connector):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Motor Pin 1 (M+)   --> Connect to L298N OUT3 (Right Screw Terminal)
  Motor Pin 2 (GND)  --> Connect to Arduino GND Pin (Bottom Row, Power Header)
  Motor Pin 3 (A)    --> Connect to Arduino Digital Pin 7 (Top Row)
  Motor Pin 4 (B)    --> Connect to Arduino Digital Pin 8 (Top Row)
  Motor Pin 5 (VCC)  --> Connect to Arduino 5V Pin (Share with Left Motor 5V)
  Motor Pin 6 (M-)   --> Connect to L298N OUT4 (Right Screw Terminal)

[C] L298N MOTOR DRIVER TO ARDUINO UNO (Logic Pins):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  L298N IN1          --> Connect to Arduino Digital Pin 9  (Left Motor Forward)
  L298N IN2          --> Connect to Arduino Digital Pin 10 (Left Motor Backward)
  L298N IN3          --> Connect to Arduino Digital Pin 11 (Right Motor Forward)
  L298N IN4          --> Connect to Arduino Digital Pin 12 (Right Motor Backward)
  L298N ENA & ENB    --> Keep black Jumper Caps ON (or connect to Pins 5 & 6)
  L298N GND Terminal --> Connect to Arduino GND Pin (Top Row, next to Pin 13)

[D] POWER & BATTERY CONNECTIONS (L298N Bottom 3-Pin Screw Terminal):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Battery (+) [Red Wire, 7.4V - 12V]  --> Connect to L298N +12V Terminal
  Battery (-) [Black Wire]            --> Connect to L298N GND Terminal
  Arduino GND Jumper Wire             --> Connect to L298N GND Terminal
  5V Regulator Jumper (Black Cap)     --> Keep ON (behind screw terminals)
  Arduino Power                       --> USB Cable connected to Raspberry Pi

4. EMERGENCY TROUBLESHOOTING & CHECKLIST
================================================================================
  1. If wheels don't spin: Check if battery power switch is ON and RED LED on
     the L298N board is illuminated.
  2. Verify that Arduino GND is connected to the L298N GND screw terminal.
  3. If a wheel spins backward on Forward (W): Swap the two wires in OUT1/OUT2
     (for Left Motor) or OUT3/OUT4 (for Right Motor).
