2. ARDUINO UNO + L298N DRIVER PINOUT MATRIX
================================================================================
[LEFT MOTOR (OUT1 / OUT2)]
* L298N ENA  --> Arduino Digital Pin D5 (or Jumper Cap ON for 100% Full Speed)
* L298N IN1  --> Arduino Digital Pin D9  (Left Motor Forward)
* L298N IN2  --> Arduino Digital Pin D10 (Left Motor Backward)
* L298N OUT1 --> Left Motor Pin 1 (Power M1)
* L298N OUT2 --> Left Motor Pin 6 (Power M2)
* Left Encoder A (C1) --> Arduino Digital Pin D2 (Interrupt)
* Left Encoder B (C2) --> Arduino Digital Pin D3 (Interrupt)

[RIGHT MOTOR (OUT3 / OUT4)]
* L298N ENB  --> Arduino Digital Pin D6 (or Jumper Cap ON for 100% Full Speed)
* L298N IN3  --> Arduino Digital Pin D11 (Right Motor Forward)
* L298N IN4  --> Arduino Digital Pin D12 (Right Motor Backward)
* L298N OUT3 --> Right Motor Pin 1 (Power M1)
* L298N OUT4 --> Right Motor Pin 6 (Power M2)
* Right Encoder A (C1) --> Arduino Digital Pin D4
* Right Encoder B (C2) --> Arduino Digital Pin D7

================================================================================
3. POWER & COMMON GROUND
================================================================================
* 12V Battery (+)  --> L298N +12V / VS (Left Blue Screw Terminal)
* 12V Battery (-)  --> L298N GND       (Middle Blue Screw Terminal)
* Arduino GND Pin  --> L298N GND       (Middle Blue Screw Terminal)
  *** CRITICAL: Arduino GND and Battery (-) MUST be connected together! ***
* L298N 5V Regulator Jumper Cap --> PLUGGED ON (Behind blue screw terminals)
